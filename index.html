Alpaca Sudoku Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-10-08

This policy explains what the Android app Alpaca Sudoku (app ID com.alpaca.sudoku) does with your information. The short version: there is no account, we do not sell data, and the app only collects what it needs to run, to fix crashes, to count how the game is used, to show an optional ad, and to process a purchase.

1. Who we are

Alpaca Sudoku is made by Alpaca ("we", "us"). Contact: alpaca@vcoelho.com

2. No account

You do not create an account or sign in. We do not ask for your name, email address, phone number, or location.

3. What stays on your device

The app saves your games in progress, your stats and results, and your settings (such as vibration and sound) in the app's private storage on your phone. We do not receive this data. It is removed when you uninstall the app or clear its storage in Android settings. You can also clear your stats at any time in Settings > Reset stats.

4. What is collected, and why

Usage analytics (Google Firebase Analytics)

The app sends a small number of anonymous events about how the game is used: a puzzle was started, completed, restarted or paused, a hint was used, or a setting was changed. Each event carries only game details such as difficulty, whether the puzzle was timed or the daily puzzle, the outcome, elapsed time and number of hints. We use this to see which parts of the game are played and to improve it. Firebase Analytics is configured in the app so that it does not read your Android advertising ID and does not use data for ad personalisation. Firebase assigns a random app-instance identifier and may log basic device information such as device model, OS version, app version and approximate region derived from IP address.

Crash reports (Google Firebase Crashlytics)

If the app crashes, a report is sent that includes the error details, the app version, and device and OS information (such as model, OS version and memory state). We use it only to find and fix bugs.

Ads (Google AdMob)

The app shows an ad only if you choose to watch a rewarded ad to get an extra hint. There are no banner or other ads. To load and show the ad, Google AdMob may use your device's advertising ID, IP address and device information. Where the law requires it (for example in the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom), the app first shows Google's consent form (Google's User Messaging Platform) and requests ads only as your choice allows. We do not separately mark the app as directed to children; ads follow Google's consent rules.

You can review or change your ad choice at any time in Settings > Privacy choices (shown when the law requires it). You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android settings.

Purchases (Google Play Billing and RevenueCat)

If you buy a subscription or the one-time unlock, the payment is handled by Google Play. We never see or receive your card or payment details. We use RevenueCat to check and restore what you have bought. RevenueCat receives an anonymous app user ID it generates for the install, your purchase records (what you bought and when) and basic device information, so it can tell whether the unlock is active.

5. Third parties

These providers process data for us under their own policies:

  • Google Firebase (Analytics and Crashlytics): firebase.google.com/support/privacy
  • Google AdMob and Google's consent form: policies.google.com/privacy
  • RevenueCat: revenuecat.com/privacy
  • Google Play (store and billing): policies.google.com/privacy

6. We do not sell your data

We do not sell your personal information and we do not share it with anyone except the providers listed above, who process it to provide their services.

7. Children

Alpaca Sudoku is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. Ads follow Google's consent rules. If you believe a child has given us information, contact us and we will help get it deleted.

8. Retention

We keep no personal data ourselves beyond what the providers above hold. Firebase, AdMob and RevenueCat keep data for the periods set in their own policies and settings. Local data on your phone stays until you reset your stats or uninstall the app. Because we hold no account or name that links these records to you, we may not be able to find a specific person's records; if you want data about your install deleted, contact us with your request and we will do what we can through the providers.

9. Your choices

  • Clear your stats: Settings > Reset stats.
  • Change ad consent: Settings > Privacy choices.
  • Reset your advertising ID: Android Settings > Privacy (or Google > Ads).
  • Remove all local data: uninstall the app.

10. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will post the new version at this address and update the effective date above. Material changes will also be noted in the app's release notes.
